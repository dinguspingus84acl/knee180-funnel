The side most people have never had explained to them

Your gut is lined with small patches of immune tissue called Peyer's patches. Think of them as security checkpoints. They "decide" what your body should react to and what it should leave alone. Scientists call this oral tolerance.

This is the "response side": not how much material reaches your joints, but how your body responds. And research suggests one ingredient talks to it directly: undenatured type II collagen.

"Undenatured" means its natural folded shape is intact. That shape is what the checkpoints are thought to recognize, which may help support your body's normal joint response, for knees that feel more comfortable and flexible when you move.*
