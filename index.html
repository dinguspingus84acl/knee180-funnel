The "hidden" route: how a 40 mg tablet works through your gut

The ingredient is undenatured type II collagen, made from chicken sternum cartilage with a low-temperature process. "Undenatured" means its natural, folded shape stays intact. That shape is the whole point.

Here's the surprising part. It isn't meant to be "building material" for your joints at all. It's more like a message.

Your gut is lined with small patches of immune tissue called Peyer's patches. Think of them as security checkpoints that decide what your immune system should react to and what it should leave alone. Scientists call that process oral tolerance.

Research suggests the intact shape of undenatured type II collagen is "recognized" at those checkpoints, which may help support the body's normal joint response, so your knees stay more comfortable and flexible when you move.*

That's why the dose is tiny. You don't need a mountain of material to deliver a message.
